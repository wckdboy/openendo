## Headlines this week

### 1. Computational repurposing proof-of-concept: simvastatin + primaquine
**Oskotsky TT, Tang X, Arthurs E et al.** "A transcriptomics-based
computational drug repurposing pipeline identifies simvastatin and
primaquine as therapeutics for endometriosis." *iScience*, 2026-09-18.
PMID 42668641 · doi:10.1016/j.isci.2026.117153
- Transcriptomics-driven pipeline over approved drugs → **simvastatin**
  (statin, anti-inflammatory/anti-angiogenic) and **primaquine**
  (anti-malarial, oxidative stress) flagged as candidates

### 2. SEMA3C → ferroptosis axis (mechanistic lead)
SEMA3C promotes endometriosis progression by inducing ferroptosis
(2026-09-01). GPX4 (in our novel-target list!) is a ferroptosis gatekeeper
— **direct overlap between this week's mechanism literature and our
target audit.** Worth a targeted follow-up.

### 3. Shared genetic architecture, female reproduction
(2026-09-01) — shared genetic architecture + cellular convergence between
female reproductive traits and endometriosis. Supports the registry/
genetics angle (agenda: "-omics integration").

### 4. Diversity gap in endometriosis evidence
(2026-09-01) — lack of socio-demographic participant diversity in
endometriosis evidence. Implication for the hub: our data collection

## M3 repurposing screen — first results (2026-09-02)

`scripts/repurpose_screen.py`: approved drugs (max_phase 4) with measured
potency (pchembl ≥ 6) against the **35 novel targets** → **9 candidate pairs**
(docs/data/repurposing_candidates.json):

| Target | Approved drug(s) | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| **SLC7A11** | **Sulfasalazine** | xCT/cystine-glutamate antiporter = ferroptosis gatekeeper — converges with this week's SEMA3C→ferroptosis paper; sulfasalazine is a known xCT inhibitor |
| **FKBP4** | Sirolimus, Tacrolimus, Cyclosporine | FKBP4 is the progesterone-receptor chaperone; endometriosis is progesterone-resistant — mechanism-relevant |
| **MRGPRX2** | Cetrorelix | GnRH antagonists are already used in endometriosis — supports target validity |
| **GPER1** | Estradiol | GPER1 is the rapid-signaling estrogen receptor — validates target in an estrogen-driven disease |
| **ACVR1B** | Crizotinib, Dabrafenib | TGF-β/activin signaling (fibrosis, lesion growth) |
| **SLCO2A1** | Dinoprostone (PGE2) | prostaglandin transporter — direction of effect needs review (agonist vs blockade) |

Caveats: in-vitro potency ≠ clinical efficacy; direction of effect per
target requires mechanism review; hypothesis-generation only. Next:
cross-reference expression (GTEx) + pathway direction before any clinical
follow-up. The Oskotsky simvastatin/primaquine pipeline remains the
transcriptomics-side template; this screen is the target-activity-side
complement.
