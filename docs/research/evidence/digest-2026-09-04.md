## Headlines this week

### 1. Dienogest real-world patient data (clinically actionable)
Real-world effectiveness and tolerability of dienogest in endometriosis —
cross-sectional patient-reported study (Prost et al., J Gynecol Obstet
Hum Reprod, 2026-09-03). PMID 42692407. Directly relevant to the Danish
standard-of-care context and to the ryeqo/yselty treatment landscape on
the wiki: patient-reported outcomes for the most-prescribed
progestin-based therapy.

### 2. Peritoneal hypoxia: stabilizes, not initiates, lesions
"Human peritoneal hypoxia may stabilize rather than initiate endometriotic
lesions" (2026-09-01). PMID 42679384. Refines the lesion-biology model —
relevant to mechanistic framing of lesion persistence (and to why
anti-oxidant/ferroptosis logic must be compartment-specific; cf.
`ferroptosis-direction.md`).

### 3. Exosomal miR-22-3p → p53 axis (new ncRNA mechanism)
Exosomal miR-22-3p targets p53 to regulate endometrial epithelial cell
function in endometriosis pathogenesis (J Mol Histol, 2026-09-04).
PMID 42693330. Adds to the non-coding-RNA mechanism map; candidate
biomarker/signaling thread for the -omics integration agenda.

### 4. Fertility preservation: AMH decline after cystectomy
Risk factors for marked decline in anti-Müllerian hormone after minimally
invasive ovarian cystectomy (2026-08-28). PMID 42675751. Patient-relevant
for endometrioma surgery decisions; pairs with the unilateral-endometrioma
ovarian-response systematic review (2026-08-31).

### 5. Endometriosis and elite sports — a blind spot
(2026-09-01). PMID 42680575. Awareness/advocacy gap — aligns with the
patient-first agenda and the "invisible disease" framing.
