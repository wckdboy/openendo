# Evidence digest — 2026-09-11 (week 2026-09-04..09-11)

18 new endometriosis papers indexed this week in PubMed. The week is
clinically thinner than the last two (no new trial readouts in the top
journals) but it is unusually strong on two things OpenEndo tracks: **pain
biology** (why lesions hurt in some patients and not others) and **Danish
genetics** — the 25-year synthesis of endometriosis GWAS from the QIMR /
Aalborg / Statens Serum Institut group lands in *Human Reproduction
Update*. Mechanism papers cluster again around ferroptosis, epigenetics
and progesterone resistance. Prior digest: 2026-09-04.
Every entry below comes from a fetched PubMed record; PMIDs are live.

## Diagnostics

- **#Enzian by transvaginal ultrasound in 394 consecutive patients** —
  deep endometriosis in 33.5%, ovarian endometriosis in 35.8%; the
  uterosacral ligament / pelvic sidewall (B) compartment was the most
  frequent deep site (52.9%) and rectal involvement (C) followed at 31.0%.
  Laterality is compartment-specific (tubo-ovarian adhesions bilateral in
  63.1%). Retrospective single-operator cohort, specialised centre.
  [PMID 42717817](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42717817/) — *why it
  matters:* gives real-world distribution baselines for the IDEA/#Enzian
  ultrasound pathway, the non-surgical route to lesion mapping that
  OpenEndo's access content leans on.
- **Serum ANGPTL4 as a candidate blood marker** — serum (and peritoneal
  fluid) ANGPTL4 was higher in surgically confirmed endometriosis than in
  controls (512.7 ± 46.9 vs 177.6 ± 11.8 ng/mL, p<0.001); a 188.61 ng/mL
  cut-off gave AUC 0.755, sensitivity 75%, specificity 62.5%.
  Prospective case–control study, single centre, 2× groups.
  [PMID 42694335](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42694335/) — *why it
  matters:* specificity is too low for a stand-alone diagnostic, but it is
  another entry for the non-invasive-marker watchlist (alongside CA125,
  which also separated the groups here).
- **Intraoperative ultrasound to localise deep rectosigmoid nodules** —
  technique note from Mayo Clinic Arizona on using intraoperative US for
  nodule localisation during surgery. Technical report, no outcome data.
  [PMID 42702235](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42702235/) — *why it
  matters:* surgical-precision item only; no evidence change.

## Treatment and drugs

- **Relugolix combination therapy — 104-week SPIRIT extension** —
  post hoc analysis of SPIRIT 1/2 and their long-term extension
  (802/1261 entered the LTE, 501 completed 104 weeks) tracking cumulative
  probability and median time to minimal-to-no dysmenorrhea and
  non-menstrual pelvic pain, amenorrhea, and analgesic-free status.
  Industry-sponsored post hoc analysis of RCT data.
  [PMID 42719373](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42719373/) — *why it
  matters:* the longest follow-up yet on an oral GnRH-antagonist
  add-back regimen; the timeline-to-benefit numbers are the practical
  part for shared decision-making.
- **M1 macrophage nanovesicles delivering bazedoxifene to lesions** —
  CCL2–CCR2-targeted M1 membrane nanovesicles carrying the estrogen-
  receptor antagonist bazedoxifene reduced inflammatory infiltration,
  angiogenesis and fibrosis in a preclinical model without ovarian or
  uterine toxicity. Preclinical (cell/animal) formulation study.
  [PMID 42702982](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42702982/) — *why it
  matters:* a lesion-targeted route around systemic hormonal suppression;
  early, but it speaks to the same local-delivery logic OpenEndo's
  repurposing track considers.
- **FIN56-induced ferroptosis via ACACA/ARID5A/NOX4** — ferroptosis
  inducer FIN56 suppressed ectopic lesion growth in vitro/in vivo by
  raising mitochondrial ROS and lipid peroxidation through an
  ACC1(ACACA)–ARID5A–NOX4 axis. Preclinical mechanistic study (ectopic
  endometrial stromal cells + model). [PMID 42698143](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42698143/)
  — *why it matters:* third independent confirmation this year that
  ferroptosis induction is a real therapeutic direction in endometriosis
  — consistent with `ferroptosis-direction.md` and with the SLC7A11 /
  xCT selectivity caveat that keeps sulfasalazine on WATCHLIST.
- **HDAC inhibitors — review of the preclinical case** — abnormal HDAC
  expression drives proliferation, apoptosis evasion, inflammation and
  EMT; vorinostat, romidepsin and butyrate have preclinical activity, with
  clinical translation blocked by selectivity and delivery. Narrative
  review. [PMID 42717121](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42717121/) —
  *why it matters:* maps an epigenetic-drug class for the repurposing
  watchlist; no new human data.
- **ZEB1 as an EMT / progesterone-resistance hub** — review positioning
  ZEB1 as a transcriptional integrator of estrogen induction, PR-pathway
  suppression, miR-200/lncRNA/circRNA feedback and invasive phenotype in
  ectopic lesions. Narrative review.
  [PMID 42717122](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42717122/) — *why it
  matters:* an upstream node for the progesterone-resistance thread
  (the FKBP52/HOXA10/miR-29c axis OpenEndo already tracks); explicitly
  flags that targeting ZEB1 therapeutically is paradoxical.
- **Non-invasive brain stimulation for chronic pelvic pain** —
  systematic review of tDCS and rTMS in chronic pelvic pain: 15 studies,
  295 participants (264 completers), substantial heterogeneity, most RCTs
  at high or some risk of bias; narrative synthesis only.
  [PMID 42722127](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42722127/) — *why it
  matters:* the central-sensitisation treatment angle patients ask about;
  current evidence does not support routine use.
- **Laparoscopic discoid excision with hand-sewn bowel closure** —
  10 patients with 3–5 cm deep bowel nodules unsuitable for stapled
  discoid excision; manual two-layer suturing took a mean 22 min, one
  severe febrile morbidity, 18–60 months follow-up, no severe
  complications. Retrospective cohort (prospectively collected data),
  n=10. [PMID 42722072](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42722072/) —
  *why it matters:* a bowel-sparing alternative in the surgical debate
  over segmental resection vs shaving; very small numbers, surgeon-
  dependent.
- **Robot-assisted buccal mucosa graft ureteroplasty** — technique note
  for ureteric repair in deep infiltrating endometriosis. Case/technical
  report. [PMID 42705528](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42705528/) —
  *why it matters:* rare-complication surgical capability, relevant to the
  urinary-tract deep-endometriosis pathway.

## Mechanisms

- **Why some lesions hurt and others do not — inflammatory gene
  signature** — RNA-seq of 27 peritoneal biopsies from 9 symptomatic and
  10 asymptomatic patients: 890 differentially expressed genes, including
  inflammatory signalling (IL16, IL17RA, JAK3, SMPD3, RELT), adhesion
  (OLFML1, CDON, VCAN) and neuromodulation (SEMA6D, ADRA2C, SLC7A5); IL16
  also tracked severity in mild vs severe pain. Human tissue
  transcriptomics (Yale). [PMID 42720599](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42720599/)
  — *why it matters:* direct molecular support for the long-standing
  clinical observation that stage does not predict pain — and the first
  concrete candidate (IL16) for a symptom-severity biomarker.
- **Fibrotic substrate stiffness drives endometriotic epithelial
  motility** — on tunable polyacrylamide hydrogels, intermediate stiffness
  maximised cell speed, actin stress fibres, focal adhesions and spheroid
  expansion (biphasic dose–response). In-vitro mechanobiology.
  [PMID 42720355](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42720355/) — *why it
  matters:* frames fibrosis as an active biomechanical driver of lesion
  spread, not just scar tissue.
- **Gut microbiota-associated targets by integrative multi-omics** —
  61 metabolite-linked DEGs; MET (protective direction) and SELL (modest
  risk direction) as hub genes, with *Bacteroides*, *Parabacteroides*,
  *Bifidobacterium* and metabolites palmitoylethanolamide /
  dihydroresveratrol implicated in immune-inflammatory pathways;
  molecular-simulation binding checks. Computational multi-omics +
  single-cell reanalysis. [PMID 42713565](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42713565/)
  — *why it matters:* adds a named gut–immune axis to the microbiome
  thread; hypothesis-generating, not clinical.
- **β-Glucuronidase and the estrobolome (broader review)** — review of
  bacterial β-glucuronidase reactivating estrogens through enterohepatic
  recycling, and the >10⁴-fold selectivity possible against bacterial GH2
  over human GUSB; names endometriosis among the estrobolome-linked
  conditions. Narrative review, not endometriosis-specific.
  [PMID 42716136](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42716136/) — *why it
  matters:* the mechanistic backdrop for the gut-estrogen hypothesis
  including a precision-inhibitor angle; context only, no endo data.

## Epidemiology, genetics and access

- **Genetic architecture of endometriosis — 25-year synthesis** —
  *Human Reproduction Update* review from the QIMR Berghofer group with
  **Aalborg University and Statens Serum Institut co-authors**: from
  Treloar's 1999 twin-study heritability estimate of 51% through GWAS risk
  loci and target genes, to the use of large public genetic datasets for
  comorbidity analysis and clinical implication. Invited review.
  [PMID 42695929](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42695929/) — *why it
  matters:* the current single best map of endometriosis genetics, and it
  is co-authored from the Danish registry/biobank ecosystem — directly
  relevant to OpenEndo's Danish-registries and Danish-data workstreams.
- **Endometriosis and fertility problems in a Polish national survey** —
  cross-sectional online survey, 5,532 respondents who had tried to
  conceive; fertility problems reported by 36.6% of women; among women,
  endometriosis carried OR 2.82 (vs 23.0 for tubal obstruction, 5.35 for
  PCOS). Survey epidemiology. [PMID 42715119](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42715119/)
  — *why it matters:* population-level burden figure for a European
  cohort; useful comparator for Danish survey/registry framing.
- **Correction to the ARCHES endometriosis/type-2-diabetes cohort paper**
  — erratum (Diabetologia) for the 2026-08-06 ARCHES retrospective cohort
  study on endometriosis subtypes and type-2-diabetes risk. Erratum only;
  the corrected text is not summarised in the record.
  [PMID 42709073](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42709073/) — *why it
  matters:* if the comorbidity signal is cited anywhere (OpenEndo wiki,
  digest archives) it should be re-checked against the corrected version.

## Notable clinical signals

- **Relugolix-CT (PMID 42719373)** is the one item this week that changes
  what a clinician could say to a patient: 104-week data on time-to-
  minimal-to-no pain and analgesic-free status for an oral GnRH-antagonist
  add-back regimen. Read it as sponsor-run post hoc analysis of RCT data,
  not as an independent trial.
- **Pain-molecular signature (PMID 42720599)** is not yet clinical, but it
  is the strongest mechanistic answer this year to "why do I hurt and my
  scan doesn't show stage IV?" — IL16 is the name to watch.
- Nothing this week changes any OpenEndo repurposing verdict; the FIN56
  ferroptosis paper (PMID 42698143) reinforces the existing direction
  without altering the sulfasalazine selectivity question.
