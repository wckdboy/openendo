# Yselty (linzagolix 75/200 mg ± add-back)

Oral GnRH-antagonist with a **flexible dosing option without hormonal add-back**. See [[gnrh-antagonists]].

## Key facts
- **EU-approved for endometriosis:** 20 December 2024 (CHMP positive Oct 2024; EDELWEISS 3 evidence).^[https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/yselty]
- **NICE recommends routine NHS use** (England) with add-back therapy; ~£1,100/year list price.^[https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK616794/]
- **Evidence (EDELWEISS 3, n=486):** 200 mg + ABT — 72.9% dysmenorrhea responders vs 23.5% placebo (OR 8.80); 75 mg alone — 44.0% (OR 2.56). 75 mg works **without hormonal add-back** — relevant for women who can't tolerate hormones.^[https://doi.org/10.1093/humrep/deae076]
- **EDELWEISS 6 extension (12 months):** 91.0% dysmenorrhea responders on 200 mg + ABT; efficacy sustained.^[https://read.qxmd.com/read/42077502]
- **Danish availability: UNVERIFIED ⚠️** — the Danish reimbursement council decision (Nov 2023) covered fibroids only; Theramex launched first in DE/ES/PL/IT/UK. Ask the pharmacy/specialist whether it can be procured in DK.

## Why it matters
The 75 mg no-add-back option is clinically unique — but in Denmark, [[ryeqo]] is the practical first choice today (reimbursed, in stock).
