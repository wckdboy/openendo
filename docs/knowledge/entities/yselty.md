# Yselty (linzagolix 100/200 mg)

Oral GnRH-antagonist. See [[gnrh-antagonists]].

## Key facts (verified 2026-09-02)

- **EU approval, uterine fibroids:** 14 June 2022 (central procedure).^[EMA]
- **EU approval, endometriosis:** 20 December 2024 — *"symptomatic treatment of endometriosis in women with a history of previous medical or surgical treatment for their endometriosis"* (CHMP positive October 2024). Same second-line positioning as [[ryeqo]].^[Theramex EC announcement]
- **Endometriosis dose (EU SmPC):** 200 mg once daily **with** hormonal add-back therapy (estradiol 1 mg / norethisterone acetate 0.5 mg), taken continuously. The 100 mg-no-add-back flexibility exists only on the fibroid label.^[EMA product information]
- **Evidence — EDELWEISS 3 (+ EDELWEISS 6 extension):** 484 women with surgically confirmed endometriosis, randomised to placebo, Yselty 75 mg, or Yselty 200 mg + ABT. Dysmenorrhea responders: 72.9% vs 23.5% placebo at month 3 (OR 8.80; 97.5% CI 4.86–15.91), sustained at month 12 (91.0%). Non-menstrual pelvic pain responders: 47.3% vs 30.9% (OR 2.01).^[EMA product information §5.1]
- The 75 mg monotherapy arm also separated from placebo (published analysis: 44.0% dysmenorrhea responders)^[https://doi.org/10.1093/humrep/deae076] — but 75 mg is **not** a marketed endometriosis dose.
- ~98.6% of EDELWEISS participants were White; efficacy evidence in non-White populations is thin (see the diversity gap in the M2 digest).
- **England:** NICE guidance TA1067 recommends linzagolix as an option for endometriosis symptoms.^[NICE]

## Denmark — verified status (2026-09-02)

- **Authorised** in Denmark via the EU central procedure. Lægemiddelstyrelsen expected it on the Danish market from September 2024; dispensing group **NBS** (specialist gyn/obs).^[Medicintilskudsnævnet indstilling]
- **Reimbursement:** *generelt klausuleret tilskud* (decision process Nov–Dec 2023) covers **uterine fibroids only**, with a clause — patients with moderate-to-severe fibroid symptoms where GnRH-antagonist combined with norethisterone/estrogen (with general, unclaused reimbursement) is not medically appropriate. The doctor must write *"tilskud"* on the prescription.^[Medicintilskudsnævnet indstilling]
- **No general reimbursement for the endometriosis indication as of 2026-09-02** — no Lægemiddelstyrelsen decision found. Endometriosis treatment is therefore **out-of-pocket** at the pharmacy, unless the region grants *enkelttilskud* (individual reimbursement) on application — ask the specialist.
- Danish list price is not published in the LMS documents (confidential). UK list price ~£1,100/year (NICE). Check the current Danish pack price before relying on cost estimates.

## Why it matters

For Danish endometriosis patients, [[ryeqo]] is the reimbursed first choice. Yselty matters when add-back-containing GnRH-antagonist therapy is unsuitable — but for the endometriosis indication it currently means full price, specialist prescription, and a pharmacy-level availability check. Verify current stock and price with the pharmacy before planning around it.
