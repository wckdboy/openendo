# MY-ENDO — digital mindfulness & acceptance-based self-management (Denmark)

*"Mind Your ENDOmetriosis."* A non-drug clinical study testing whether a **digital mindfulness- and acceptance-based programme** improves quality of life, work ability, pain and mental/physical health in people with endometriosis. Run by **Aarhus University** with Aarhus University Hospital and the Danish endometriosis patient organisation.^[https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06211231]

Featured on the OpenEndo homepage under "Take action" — recruiting in Denmark.^[https://openendo.org/index.html]

## Who can join (plain-language summary)

**Inclusion:**
- ≥ 18 years, informed consent.
- Endometriosis diagnosed by a gynaecologist (medical history, ultrasound, laparoscopy or MRI — self-reported at screening).
- Moderate-to-severe symptoms: pelvic pain or fatigue > 2 on the 0–10 scale, **or** reduced endometriosis-related quality of life (EHP-30 subscale ≥ 40).
- Relevant medical and/or surgical treatment per ESHRE guidelines must already have been tried.
- Willing to practise mindfulness and yoga at home **30–45 minutes, 5–7 days a week** during the treatment period.

**Exclusion:**
- Severe psychiatric diagnosis (made by a psychiatrist) or ongoing psychiatric treatment.
- Pregnancy, or planned pregnancy during the study.
- Circumstances that would block participation: major life events (e.g. divorce, bereavement) or linguistic/cultural barriers.

## Why it matters

Most endometriosis trials test drugs. MY-ENDO tests a **non-drug, digital self-management tool** — directly relevant to the hub's patient-first agenda, and a rare Danish recruiting option for people who have already tried guideline treatment and still live with pain/fatigue. It complements (does not replace) medical care.^[https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06211231]

Talk to your gynaecologist or the study team before deciding. Registry data for this trial is also in the site's weekly dataset.^[https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wckdboy/openendo/main/docs/data/trials_denmark.json]
