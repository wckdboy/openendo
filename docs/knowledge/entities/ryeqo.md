# Ryeqo (relugolix 40 mg + estradiol 1 mg + norethisterone acetate 0.5 mg)

Oral GnRH-antagonist combination — one pill daily. See [[gnrh-antagonists]].

## Key facts
- **EU-approved for symptomatic endometriosis:** November 2023, for women with a history of previous medical or surgical treatment.^[https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/ryeqo]
- **Available in Denmark with generelt tilskud** (general reimbursement — automatic at the pharmacy). The Danish Medicines Agency (Lægemiddelstyrelsen) preserved general reimbursement for the endometriosis indication.^[https://laegemiddelstyrelsen.dk/da/nyheder/2024/ryeqo-bevarer-generelt-tilskud/]
- **Price in DK:** ~849 DKK / 28 tablets (~28–30 DKK/day); 84-tablet packs ~2,411–2,416 DKK (medicin.dk, Sep 2026).^[https://medicin.dk/Medicin/Praeparater/9905]
- **Prescribing:** dispensing group NBS (gyn/obs) — specialist-prescribed. "Previous treatment" is interpreted by the reimbursement council as prior contraceptive-pill use.
- **Evidence (SPIRIT 1+2, n=1,261, 24 weeks):** significantly more responders on dysmenorrhea and non-menstrual pelvic pain vs placebo; more women completely off analgesics at 24 weeks.
- **Add-back is built in** (estradiol + NETA) — protects bone mineral density; aligns with DSOG guidance for GnRH analogues.

## Why it matters
Ryeqo is currently the **most accessible new treatment in Denmark** for endometriosis after standard hormones have failed — approved, reimbursed, and pharmacy-available.
