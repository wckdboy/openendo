# HMI-115 (Hope Medicine)

First-in-class **anti-prolactin-receptor monoclonal antibody** — the first
**non-hormonal** therapy for endometriosis to reach Phase 3.

## Key facts
- **Mechanism:** blocks prolactin-receptor signalling implicated in endometriosis lesion growth — no hormonal suppression, so no menopausal side effects.
- **Phase 2:** significant pain reduction vs placebo **without menopausal side effects** (published in Lancet Obstetrics, Gynaecology & Women's Health).^[https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hope-medicine-announces-positive-phase-2-results]
- **Phase 3:** first patient dosed March 2026 (China); NCT07318688. FDA Fast Track designation.^[https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07318688]
- **Status confidence:** medium — pipeline claims from company materials; independent replication pending.

## Why it matters
If Phase 3 confirms Phase 2, this is the first fundamentally new treatment mechanism for endometriosis in decades — an alternative for women who cannot or will not take hormones. Realistic EU/DK timeline: earliest ~2029 if all goes well. Do not count on it today.
