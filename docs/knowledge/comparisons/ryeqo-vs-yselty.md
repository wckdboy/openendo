# Ryeqo vs Yselty — practical comparison

| Dimension | Ryeqo (relugolix combo) | Yselty (linzagolix) |
|---|---|---|
| Approval | EU Nov 2023 | EU Dec 2024 |
| Add-back | Built into the pill | Optional (75 mg can run without) |
| Evidence | SPIRIT 1+2 (n=1,261) | EDELWEISS 3+6 (n=486+353) |
| Dysmenorrhea responders | ~75% vs 27% placebo (24 wks) | 72.9% (200 mg+ABT) / 44.0% (75 mg) vs 23.5% |
| DK access | **Generelt tilskud, pharmacy-stocked** ⚠️ | Reimbursement covers fibroids only; availability unverified |
| DK cost | ~28–30 DKK/day | ~£1,100/year list (UK price) |
| No-hormone option | No | Yes (75 mg) |

## Verdict
**For a Danish patient today: Ryeqo is the practical choice** — approved,
reimbursed, available. **Yselty is the specialist choice** — the 75 mg
no-add-back option matters for women who cannot tolerate hormones, but the
Danish pathway must be probed (pharmacy/specialist) and is not yet standard.
