# GnRH antagonists — the class behind Ryeqo and Yselty

Oral, reversible suppression of the pituitary-gonadal axis. Unlike older
GnRH *agonists* (which flare first), antagonists suppress estradiol quickly
and reversibly — and unlike agonists, they can be taken as a daily pill.

## How it works
- Blocks GnRH receptors → rapid drop in estradiol → endometriosis lesions starve and shrink.
- **Add-back therapy** (low-dose estradiol + progestin) protects bone mineral density and reduces hot flushes — built into [[ryeqo]], optional with [[yselty]].
- Full suppression is not required for pain relief: partial suppression (75 mg linzagolix) works and preserves more estradiol — relevant for younger women.

## Evidence position
- Ryeqo: SPIRIT 1+2 (n=1,261) — significant, clinically meaningful response; reimbursed in DK.
- Yselty: EDELWEISS 3 + 6 (n=486 + 353) — significant; 75 mg option without add-back; NICE recommended.
- Class caveat: both are **"after previous medical or surgical treatment"** — i.e. second-line after standard hormones, not first-line.

## Open questions
- Head-to-head vs progestins/estroprogestins is largely missing (EDELWEISS 6 authors note this).^[https://read.qxmd.com/read/42077502]
- Long-term (multi-year) bone and metabolic safety data still accumulating.
