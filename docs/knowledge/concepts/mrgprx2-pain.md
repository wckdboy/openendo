# MRGPRX2 — a mast-cell pain axis in endometriosis

MRGPRX2 (Mas-related G-protein-coupled receptor X2) is a mast-cell
activation receptor. It is "not detected" in bulk normal-tissue atlases
(HPA) — which makes its role in **lesional mast cells** a specific,
testable question (OpenEndo virtual-testing Phase 0.5).

## Why it surfaced
- **Pain mechanism (2025, verified):** MRGPRX2 mediates mast-cell-induced
  endometriosis pain through sensory-neuron sensitization via the
  histamine/HRH1/TRPV1 pathway.^[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40600649/]
- **M3 screen hit:** cetrorelix (GnRH-antagonist, established endo
  treatment) shows measured potency at MRGPRX2 — but read as µM
  **agonism**, i.e. target-validating, not a repurposing signal in the
  therapeutic direction.^[https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wckdboy/openendo/main/docs/research/evidence/m3-validation.md]

## Implication
MRGPRX2 **antagonism** is a candidate non-hormonal, non-opioid pain
target for endometriosis — mechanistically distinct from every current
treatment class. Hypothesis-stage; requires lesion-level expression
confirmation (are lesional mast cells MRGPRX2⁺?) and selective-antagonist
studies.
