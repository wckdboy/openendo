# Non-invasive saliva diagnostics — ending the 7–10-year wait

The average woman waits **7–10 years** for an endometriosis diagnosis, during
which the disease spreads and fertility is affected. Diagnosis today is
surgical (laparoscopy) or ultrasound/MRI in expert hands. Saliva-based
molecular testing aims to change that.

## The test
- **Ziwig Endotest:** saliva sample → microRNA profiling + AI → result in ~1 week. CE-IVD marked, prescription-based, marketed sensitivity >97% / specificity >93% (manufacturer claims; independent validation varies).^[https://ziwig.com/en/ziwig-endotest/]
- **Available in Denmark** via Life Genomics (Sweden): +46 708-112 152, info@lifegenomics.se. Private-pay (price on enquiry). Useful when the diagnosis is still unclear — can short-circuit years of referrals.

## Validation landscape
- **GynRNA study** (ZIWIG, NCT06392997): validating salivary RNA against surgical findings — recruiting.^[https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06392997]
- EUmetriosis (Horizon Europe) is developing steroid-metabolomics + miRNA diagnostics.

## Caveats
- Not yet a diagnostic *standard*; treat as strong adjunct, not replacement for clinical work-up.
- Reimbursement: not covered by Danish public insurance — out-of-pocket.
