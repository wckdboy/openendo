# Saliva diagnostics — emerging, not a replacement for surgery

WHO reports a typical time to diagnosis of **4–12 years** (a range: “the
average time to diagnosis is between 4 and 12 years” — not a single-year
mean).^[https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/endometriosis]
Endometriosis affects an estimated **10% (190 million)** of reproductive-age
women worldwide (same fact sheet). This page is research/advocacy context,
**not medical advice**.

**Definitive diagnosis remains surgical** (laparoscopy with visualisation
and/or histology) where a tissue-level confirmation is required. Expert
ultrasound/MRI can support a clinical diagnosis. Saliva-based molecular
testing is an **emerging adjunct**, not an established diagnostic standard.

## The test
- **Ziwig Endotest:** saliva sample → microRNA profiling + AI → result in ~1 week. CE-IVD marked, prescription-based, marketed sensitivity >97% / specificity >93% (manufacturer claims; independent validation varies — do not repeat percentages as settled).^[https://ziwig.com/en/ziwig-endotest/]
- **Available in Denmark** via Life Genomics (Sweden): +46 708-112 152, info@lifegenomics.se. Private-pay (price on enquiry). Useful when the diagnosis is still unclear — can short-circuit years of referrals.

## Policy watch (2026-09)
BMJ news (Wise J, 16 Sep 2026, PMID 42749315, DOI 10.1136/bmj-2026-100876)
reports NICE recommending a single saliva test to speed diagnosis in primary
care. NCBI-verified; the PubMed record is **title-level only** (no abstract,
no accuracy figures). Treat as **NICE draft / early-use**, **emerging — not
established NHS standard**. Cite primary accuracy papers, not the news
item, before repeating any performance claim.^[https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42749315/]

## Validation landscape
- **GynRNA study** (ZIWIG, NCT06392997): validating salivary RNA against surgical findings — recruiting.^[https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06392997]
- EUmetriosis (Horizon Europe) is developing steroid-metabolomics + miRNA diagnostics.

## Caveats
- Not a diagnostic *standard*; treat as an adjunct, not a replacement for clinical work-up or surgery when definitive diagnosis is needed.
- Reimbursement: not covered by Danish public insurance — out-of-pocket.
- No established blood-test standard for endometriosis; emerging saliva tests do not change that until independently validated and commissioned.
