What we know, and how we know it.

Sourced, confidence-tagged pages on endometriosis treatment, diagnostics, research pipeline and policy. Every claim carries a source; nothing hardens into "fact" without evidence. Readable by humans here (pages open on GitHub, beautifully rendered) — and by AI agents via llms.txt.

Treatments (entities)

Ryeqo

relugolix combo · EU-approved · generelt tilskud in DK

Oral GnRH antagonist combination (relugolix + estradiol + norethisterone). EU-approved for endometriosis, maintains general reimbursement in Denmark.

confidence: high · updated 2026-09-02

Yselty

linzagolix · EU-approved Dec 2024 · no DK reimbursement for endo

Oral GnRH antagonist. EU-approved for endometriosis (Dec 2024), but Denmark's general reimbursement covers fibroids only — endometriosis treatment is out-of-pocket or via regional enkelttilskud (verified 2026-09-02).

confidence: high · updated 2026-09-02

HMI-115

anti-PRLR antibody · first non-hormonal in Phase 3

Anti-prolactin-receptor antibody, the first non-hormonal therapy to reach Phase 3 for endometriosis. Phase 2 results positive.

confidence: medium · updated 2026-09-02

Sirolimus

mTOR inhibitor · M3 repurposing rank 1 · research

Rapamycin: independent mTOR evidence in endometriosis models and a PPARα/IGFBP2 ovarian-senescence mechanism — but no registered trial in endometriosis yet. Research priority, not a recommendation.

confidence: medium · updated 2026-09-02

Trials in Denmark

MY-ENDO trial

NCT06211231 · recruiting

Digital mindfulness- and acceptance-based self-management for endometriosis pain and fatigue — Aarhus University Hospital + Danish patient organisation. Non-drug; for patients who have already tried guideline treatment.

confidence: high · updated 2026-09-02

Diversity gap

policy · advocacy · research

Under-representation of racial, ethnic and socioeconomic minority populations in endometriosis research and clinical trials. Historical "White woman's disease" framing traced to access bias, not biology. 5 sourced papers (2017-2025).

confidence: high · updated 2026-09-02

Danish registries

denmark · research · policy

Denmark's national patient, prescription, birth and psychiatric registers (CPR-linkable) are among the world's best for population-scale endometriosis research — underutilised for this disease. Key findings on diagnostic delay, obstetric outcomes and mental health burden. 7 sourced papers (2017-2026).

confidence: high · updated 2026-09-02
